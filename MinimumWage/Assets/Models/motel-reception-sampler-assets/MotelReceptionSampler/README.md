# Motel Reception & Service Props - Free Sampler

Three free props from the 17-prop Motel Reception & Service Props kit.

## Contents

- `glb/` - one glTF binary per prop, textures embedded (Y-up, metres)
- `fbx/` - one FBX per prop, textures embedded
- `obj/` - one OBJ + MTL per prop, textures alongside
- `textures/` - every texture as a PNG (5 files)
- `previews/` - one render per prop, `contact_sheet.png` shows them all
- `props.csv` - triangle count and size of every prop

## Conventions

- Metres, real-world scale. Each prop's origin is the centre of its footprint on the floor.
- The front of a prop faces +Z in the glb files (-Y in Blender).
- Wall-mounted props (none in this pack) have their back at the wall plane and are positioned at their
  real mounting height; place the origin on the floor against the wall.
- Flat shaded, no vertex colours, one material per texture, textures are 128 px or smaller. Set texture
  filtering to nearest for the intended look.
- Single-sided faces (the lampshade is open underneath); nothing uses alpha.
- FBX and OBJ keep quads; the triangle counts below are after triangulation.
